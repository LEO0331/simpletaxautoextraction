## Required rule-record fields

Rule ID/version; specific proposition and exceptions; financial years and event-date applicability; authoritative URL and section; access date and retrieval status; reviewed interpretation; reviewer and approval date; engine/fixture versions; known exclusions. Recheck the applicable compilation and amendments when approving a pack. A publication date or reachable URL is not approval.

Original Phase 1/2 coverage included reviewed ordinary AUD acquisition/disposal fees, separate bases, explicit AMIT timing/attribution and E10, linked DRP acquisitions/residual cash, and reviewed loss origins for FY2024–2025/FY2025–2026 worksheets. Subsequent extensions register year starts 2020–2025 and add bounded non-AMIT E4, evidenced splits/consolidations, reviewed AUD conversion, and source-scoped adviser decisions. See the current implementation record for integration status. Taxpayer-specific attribution, unsupported issuer layouts/events, rollovers, legal residency classification, forex elections, rental deductibility and future reforms still require review. Unsupported automatic treatment remains NeedsTaxReview; entering an adviser decision is not automatic legal coverage.
